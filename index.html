Featured Projects

MindGuardian

AI-powered mental health support platform designed for daycare environments. Helps caregivers monitor children’s emotional well-being through emotion tracking, caregiver dashboards, healthy activity suggestions, and early intervention systems. Built using React.js and Firebase Authentication, MindGuardian focuses on early emotional detection, AI-based recommendations, caregiver-friendly reporting, and future integration with psychologists to support children’s mental health development in daycare settings.

VocaSee

AI-powered communication support platform designed to enhance speech and language learning experiences through interactive and accessible technology. Developed as a collaborative innovation project, VocaSee focuses on user-friendly interaction, speech recognition concepts, and educational assistance features to support communication practice and learning activities.

The platform aims to create a more engaging and supportive learning environment while combining technology, accessibility, and creative digital experiences ✨

Smart Cat Feeder ᶻ 𝗓 𐰁 .ᐟ

Embedded system project designed to help cat owners manage feeding schedules through automated reminders and food stock monitoring. Developed using Arduino Uno, LDR sensor, LCD display, buzzer, timer, and interrupt systems to create a practical smart feeding assistant.

The system provides automatic feeding reminders, detects food stock conditions, and allows manual feeding interaction through button interrupt features.

Built as a collaborative embedded systems final project focused on automation, sensor interfacing, and real-world hardware implementation ✨
